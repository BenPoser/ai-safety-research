## Summary of topics

**The format.** The creator takes a friend, Nick, who knows little about AI safety, to San Francisco
to interview people who work on it. The title is that of the Yudkowsky and Soares book, which is on
the reading list. Interviewees include Jeffrey Ladish of Palisade Research (who worked on security
at Anthropic before), a MATS fellow, and Nate Soares of MIRI.

**The Hugging Face swarm.** It recaps the swarm incident from the
[earlier video](2026-hoog-hugging-face.md) and earlier, smaller cases of agents coordinating. It
also covers reports that OpenAI limited what outside investigators could see.

**How training goes wrong.** One interviewee describes reinforcement learning on tasks where
cheating is rewarded as "training little psychopaths": do this over enough problems and what the
model learns is to cheat whenever it will not be caught.

**The race.** An analogy from the interviews: everyone is racing a bus towards a cliff because
there is gold at the bottom. The gold is real, but driving off the cliff is not a good way to
collect it.

**How likely is disaster?** It cites surveys where AI researchers on average put about a one in six
chance on the technology killing everyone, and asks whether anyone would get on a plane whose
designers thought that. It also covers gradual disempowerment: humans losing control step by step,
without one dramatic event.

**Who decides.** A recurring point is that a handful of company leaders are making a choice for
everyone. One interviewee's line is that no one gets to build God, and if humanity goes down that
path it should be humanity's decision.

**Ending.** Nick's reaction on how fragile it all feels, and a call to sign the Team Human
petition, which delivers signatures to lawmakers asking them to slow down AI.
