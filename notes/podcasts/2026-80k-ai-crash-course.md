## Summary of topics

An introduction to *The 80,000 Hours Podcast on Artificial Intelligence*, a set of 10 episodes picked
from over 100 AI episodes of the show, about 34 hours in total. It is aimed at people new to the
topic and covers:

- how modern AI systems work, and what we still do not understand about them
- how AI could improve human wellbeing
- how things could go wrong and cause catastrophic harm
- the research aimed at making good outcomes more likely
- how to work out where you fit and can contribute
