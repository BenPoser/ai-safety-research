## Summary of topics

A debate between Robert Wright and Jeremie Harris on how the US should deal with China on AI. Wright
has long argued for US–China cooperation on AI. Harris co-founded Gladstone AI, which wrote a report
on AI risk for the US State Department, and focuses more on security and competition. The episode
title frames it with a scenario: an AI disaster happens and the US president has to call Xi
Jinping.

Topics from the show notes:

- Wright's issue with "kumbaya" as a way of describing cooperation.
- Whether it will take an "AI Chernobyl" to convince sceptics.
- Whether being hawkish on China is the safest option.
- The case for "organic transparency" with China.
- How progress on alignment could backfire.
- Whether China has infiltrated US tech.
- Dario Amodei's idea of a bloc of democracies.
